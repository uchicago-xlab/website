RISK AREAS

Four fronts of catastrophic risk.

Necessarily interdisciplinary, cutting across technology, policy, and international security.

01 — PRIMARY RISK AREAS

Artificial Intelligence

AI capabilities are developing at an exponential rate. AI experts are warning of potentially catastrophic consequences of the technology they themselves are developing, including misinformation campaigns, reduced barriers to bioterrorism, and rogue autonomous models.

READ FULL BRIEF →

Nuclear Weapons

Thousands of nuclear weapons around the world remain on hair-trigger alert — a false alarm or a single impulsive decision away from conflagration. Once launched, a missile cannot be recalled or shot down.

READ FULL BRIEF →

02 — SECONDARY RISK AREAS

Biosecurity

Rising ability to engineer pathogens, and a history of laboratory leaks, threaten outbreaks of unprecedented danger. XLab's biosecurity research program is in early development.

Climate

Feedback effects beyond a tipping point could lead to catastrophic outcomes. XLab's climate risk research program is in early development.
